## The idea
A nutrition recommendation system, from raw data to an application.

## Data science
- Merged and cleaned **5 datasets**, then encoded the features.
- Trained a `RandomForestRegressor` with **cross-validation** and **hyperparameter tuning**.
- Built analysis dashboards: heatmaps, distributions and correlations.

## What I learned
Most of the effort was in merging and cleaning heterogeneous datasets, well before any model was trained.
