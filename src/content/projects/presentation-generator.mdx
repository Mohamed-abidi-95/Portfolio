## The idea
A **Streamlit** app that turns a block of text into a **1–20 slide presentation**, exported as **PPTX or PDF**, using a **quantized local LLM** (**Qwen2.5-3B**).

## Making the output reliable
Small local models often return broken or incomplete JSON. To make every deck buildable:

- **Constrained decoding**, so the model can only produce output that matches the expected structure.
- **JSON validation with Pydantic** before any slide is rendered.

## Benchmark
We compared several open-source models on what matters for this task:

- rate of **valid JSON**,
- quality of **diagrams**,
- **latency**.

## What I learned
With small models, reliability comes from constraining and validating the output, not from hoping the model behaves.
