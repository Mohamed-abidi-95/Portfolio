## The idea
Workers and HR staff in Tunisia ask legal questions in **French, Arabic, Tunisian dialect or Arabizi** (Arabic written with Latin letters). LegalTN answers them from the **Tunisian Labor Code**, always citing the source article, and **abstains** when it does not know.

## Building the corpus
- Extracted around **500 articles per language** from a **291-page PDF** with OCR.
- **Automatic AR/FR alignment** so each Arabic article is linked to its French version.

## Search engine
- **BM25** retrieval with **abstention** and **source citation**: no LLM, so no invented answers.
- Questions accepted in French, Arabic, Tunisian dialect or Arabizi.

## Multilingual preprocessing
- **Arabizi transliteration** and **Arabic normalization**.
- Spelling correction with **SymSpell**.
- Named-entity recognition with **CRF**.

## Quality
The project ships with **79 automated tests**.

## What I learned
For legal content, a precise retriever that cites the article and knows when to say "I don't know" is more useful than a fluent generator.
