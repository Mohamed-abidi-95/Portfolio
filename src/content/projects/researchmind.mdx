## The idea
ResearchMind AI is a multimodal RAG SaaS platform for scientific content, built as an ESPRIM integrated project by a team of five. I led the **video module**: asking questions about a video and getting an extractive answer **cited to the second**.

## Pipeline
1. **Transcription**: local speech-to-text with **faster-whisper** on GPU.
2. **Chunking**: timestamped chunks, so every passage keeps its position in the video.
3. **Hybrid search**: **BM25** + **multilingual E5** embeddings, merged with **Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF)**.
4. **Reranking**: a **cross-encoder** reorders the best candidates.
5. **Generation**: a fully local LLM (**Qwen 2.5 3B** via **Ollama**).
6. **Verification**: evidence sentences are checked, and answers are **extractive** and cited to the second.

## Evaluation
I built an evaluation protocol instead of judging answers by eye:

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Recall@3 | **0.95** |
| MRR on the hard set | **0.85** |
| Off-topic questions rejected | **100%**, with no false rejections |

## Engineering
The module is integrated in the platform with **FastAPI** and **Next.js**, uses **SQLAlchemy**, ships with **35 automated tests** and runs in **Docker**.

## What I learned
A RAG system is only as good as its evaluation. Measuring retrieval (Recall@k, MRR) and testing refusals on off-topic questions told me far more than reading a few nice answers.
