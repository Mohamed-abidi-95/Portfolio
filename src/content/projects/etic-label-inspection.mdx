## The problem
Checking labels on a production line by eye is slow and error-prone. The goal was a prototype that reads and checks label content automatically from camera images.

## Pipeline
1. **Acquisition**: image from the camera.
2. **Calibration & correction**: camera calibration and lens-distortion correction for image-based metrology.
3. **Preprocessing**: adaptive thresholding, denoising and orientation correction, to cope with varying angles and lighting.
4. **Text detection**: EAST locates text regions.
5. **Reading**: Tesseract OCR extracts the text; QR codes are decoded with OpenCV.
6. **Monitoring**: a .NET 8 / WinForms desktop application shows results and real-time KPIs.

## Technology
| Technology | Role |
|---|---|
| OpenCV / OpenCvSharp4 | Preprocessing, calibration, QR decoding |
| EAST | Text region detection |
| Tesseract | OCR on detected regions |
| C# / .NET 8 (WinForms) | Desktop app with real-time KPIs |

## What I learned
On real industrial images, lighting, angle and camera geometry matter as much as the model. Calibration and preprocessing did most of the work.
